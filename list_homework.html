Mac and Cheese Recipe

I chose this recipe because I like mac and cheese 🤤

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces elbow macaroni (or other short pasta shapes like shells or cavatappi)
  • ¼ cup unsalted butter
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour (to thicken the sauce)
  • 2 cups whole milk (or half-and-half for extra richness)
  • 2 cups shredded Cheddar cheese (sharp or medium cheddar works best)
  • ½ teaspoon salt and ground black pepper (to taste)

Instructions

  1. Boil and drain the macaroni.
  2. Melt 2 tbsp butter, stir in 2 tbsp flour, and heat for 1 minute.
  3. Whisk in 1.5 cups milk until thickened.
  4. Turn off heat and stir in 2 cups shredded cheese.
  5. Stir in the cooked pasta until coated.
